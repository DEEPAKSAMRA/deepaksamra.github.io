Privacy Policy

App: Photo Sign Resizer (Android)  ·  Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: your photos and signatures are resized inside your phone. The app does not upload them, and we do not collect, see or store them. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show those ads.

1. Who we are

This app is published by DEEPAK KUMAR MEENA ("we"). Contact: junctionteacher@gmail.com

2. Photos and signatures

  • Photos you pick from the gallery or take with the camera are processed only on your device.
  • They are never sent to us or to any server. We have no server for this app.
  • The resized file is saved to your phone (folder Pictures/PhotoResizer) only when you tap Save.
  • A file leaves the app only when you yourself use Share and choose another app.
  • The app does not keep your photos after you close it.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: only to take a photo or signature picture inside the app when you choose Camera. You can use the app without it by picking photos from the gallery.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): only to save the resized file to your Pictures folder.
  • Internet: only to load ads.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect:

  • the device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • how you interact with the app and with ads (for example app launch and taps);
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance.

This data goes to Google, not to us. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads.

5. What we do not do

  • No account or login.
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts or location.
  • We do not sell any data.

6. Children

The app is meant for students and adults filling application forms. It is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: junctionteacher@gmail.com

आपकी photo और हस्ताक्षर आपके phone के अंदर ही छोटे किए जाते हैं। App उन्हें कहीं upload नहीं करता और हम उन्हें न देखते हैं, न जमा करते हैं। तैयार file आपके phone के Pictures/PhotoResizer folder में तभी save होती है जब आप Save दबाते हैं।
